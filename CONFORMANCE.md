## 3. Who Tested, on Which Device and Screen Reader, and What They Could Not Do
- **Tester & Environment:** Tested by developer and co-designer simulation protocol on Desktop Linux/Windows (Chromium + keyboard-only unplugged mouse + NVDA screen reader + Blind+ spatial earcon/auto-voice mode) and mobile viewport emulation (`320px` width at `200%` zoom, Slow 3G network throttling, and aeroplane offline mode).
- **What Worked Unaided:** Playing/pausing and seeking by keyboard or voice commands (`V`), hopping chapters (`1–6`) and sentences (`[` / `]`), inspecting current visual frames (`I`), switching between Live Real-Time EthSL Sign Translation and Synchronized Video PiP (`S`), toggling YouTube-style dual `EN+አማ` captions (`C`), inspecting 6-dot tactile Braille cells, downloading `.brf` Braille and the `< 300 KB` 3G offline ZIP pack, listening to the `<db-job-card>` audio summary, submitting a private interview accommodation request, and verifying Open Badges 3.0 credentials.
- **What Could Not Be Done / Known Device Limitation:** On Android phones that do not have an offline Amharic (`am-ET`) TTS voice installed in the OS, `window.speechSynthesis` falls back to English phonetics for spoken Amharic strings; learners on those devices rely on our Web Audio spatial earcons, the bundled recorded MP3 audio track (`va-future-audio.mp3`), the Amharic WebVTT captions/transcript, or the Ethiopian Sign Language window.
